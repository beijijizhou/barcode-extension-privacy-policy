Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Overview

This Chrome extension is designed to assist authorized users with barcode scanning, inventory tracking, quality control, and operational workflows.

Information We Collect

The extension may collect and process the following information:

  • User account information required for authentication.
  • Barcode data scanned through supported webpages.
  • Scan timestamps and related operational records.
  • Technical logs necessary for troubleshooting and system maintenance.

How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

  • Record and process barcode scans.
  • Support inventory, logistics, and quality-control operations.
  • Authenticate authorized users.
  • Maintain, monitor, and improve system performance.

Data Storage

Barcode scan records and related operational information may be transmitted securely to company-managed servers and stored in company databases for business purposes.

Data Sharing

Information collected by this extension is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be accessed only by authorized personnel and service providers necessary to operate and maintain the system.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures are implemented to protect collected information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary to support operational, legal, or business requirements.

User Rights

Users may contact their system administrator or the organization operating this extension regarding questions about their information.

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
